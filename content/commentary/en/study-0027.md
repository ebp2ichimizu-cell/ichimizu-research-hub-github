# Effects of Visible Policing on Public Reassurance and Trust
## Seeing police and understanding why they are present are not the same exposure

> **About this commentary**  
> This page is an independent Research Hub commentary based on the cited source material. It distinguishes reported findings, authors’ interpretations and Research Hub commentary, and should be read alongside the original source.


During the 2024 Common Test weekend, Saitama Prefectural Police deployed about 100 uniformed officers at 41 stations and on trains as an anti-groping measure. Officers also held posters communicating the purpose of the operation.

A two-wave online survey of 13,760 residents in Tokyo and three neighboring prefectures measured police competence, integrity, shared values, reassurance, trust and satisfaction.

The final analysis included **2,198** rail users.

## Check the statistics

Exposure:
- saw police officers: 16.7%
- noticed poster: 4.6%

Regression coefficients controlling for pre-intervention scores:

| Outcome | Saw officer | Noticed poster |
|---|---:|---:|
| competence | .13 ** | .22 ** |
| integrity | .08 * | .19 ** |
| shared values | .09 * | .28 *** |
| reassurance | .13 † | .26 * |
| trust | .09 n.s. | .36 ** |
| satisfaction | .01 n.s. | .44 ** |

The results suggest that simply seeing police was more strongly related to evaluations of police competence and integrity than to reassurance, while noticing the message was associated with a broader range of positive outcomes.

However, poster exposure was not randomized and only 4.6% noticed it.

## Research Hub commentary

Police visibility is ambiguous: it can signal protection or signal danger.

A message explaining **why police are present and what values they share with the public** may shape how that visibility is interpreted.

The study evaluates perceptions, not crime reduction.

## Limitations

- natural exposure rather than randomized assignment;
- one-page conference abstract;
- very short follow-up;
- no incident outcome.

## Key takeaway

Count not only deployed officers but also **message reach and interpretation**.
