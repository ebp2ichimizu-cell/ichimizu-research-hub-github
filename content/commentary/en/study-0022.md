# Use of Nuisance-Call Prevention Functions Among Telephone-Subsidy Recipients

> **About this commentary**  
> This page is an independent commentary prepared by the Research Hub to help police practitioners and crime-prevention professionals understand the study and its evidential limits. It is not an explanation provided or endorsed by the original authors. Please consult the original source for the exact study details.

This process evaluation asks whether older adults actually use protective telephone functions after receiving a purchase subsidy.

460 recipients responded (86.6%).

<!-- STATS_DETAIL_START -->

## Check the statistics

Current use:
- answering machine: 357/460, 83.8%
- automatic call recording: 294/423, 69.5%
- call blocking: 198/417, 47.5%

Recommendation intention was associated with automatic recording, χ²(2)=13.636, p<.01, and call blocking, χ²(2)=8.762, p<.05.

Who configured the telephone was not significantly associated with later use.

<!-- STATS_DETAIL_END -->

## Research Hub commentary

A subsidy is not an outcome. The implementation pathway is **purchase → configure → understand → continue using → reduce exposure**.
